What are we doing now?

Now that we're firmly into our mid-life phase, it's time to get back to the adventures of our 20s and 30s but with slightly better quality hotels. Not that we haven't been traveling and living in different countries over the years, but we now plan to make the most of reducing our work life so we have more time to travel. Remote working also helps! 😊

This section of YearAway captures our renewed commitment to adventure - from planning extended trips to sharing the lessons we've learned along the way. We're embracing this new chapter where work-life balance finally tips in favor of life, and we can pursue the kind of immersive travel experiences that shaped our earlier years.

Whether you're looking for travel inspiration, practical tips for mid-life adventuring, or just want to follow along with our current story, you'll find it all here. Join us as we rediscover the world with the wisdom of experience and the freedom of reduced commitments.
